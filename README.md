# Manzil 🧭
### AI-Powered University Application Roadmap Platform

> Built for ambitious students in Uzbekistan ready to take on the world's top universities.

---

## What is Manzil?

**Manzil** is an AI-powered platform that helps 9th and 10th grade students in Uzbekistan build a personalized, month-by-month roadmap for applying to top universities abroad — while tracking scholarships, olympiads, exchange programs, camps, and deadlines along the way.

---

## The Problem

Thousands of students in Uzbekistan dream of studying in:

**USA · UK · Germany · South Korea · Japan · China · Canada** · and beyond

Yet most arrive at application season completely unprepared — not from lack of ambition, but **lack of direction**. They miss scholarship deadlines, skip critical prep steps, and have no structured path forward.

---

## The Solution

Manzil solves this with four core pillars:

| | |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ **Personalized Roadmaps** | Month-by-month plans tailored to each student |
| 🤖 **AI-Generated Plans** | Smart recommendations based on student profile |
| 📅 **Opportunity Tracking** | Scholarships, olympiads, exchanges — never miss a deadline |
| 🌍 **Local Context** | Realistic, Uzbekistan-aware guidance |

---

## How It Works

### 1. Student Inputs Their Profile
- Current grade
- GPA
- Target countries & intended major
- Extracurricular activities
- English level
- Available study hours per week

### 2. AI Generates a Personalized Roadmap

The roadmap is divided into **3 tracks**:

```
📚 Academics        →  Test prep, coursework, GPA improvement
🎯 Extracurriculars →  Clubs, projects, volunteering, leadership
🏆 Honors           →  Olympiads, competitions, awards, recognition
```

### 3. Students Receive Matched Opportunities

The AI suggests universities, scholarships, and programs based on:
- GPA and academic performance
- Extracurricular activities
- Volunteering history
- Target countries and major

### 4. Students Track & Adapt

Students can:
- ✅ Mark tasks as completed
- 📊 Monitor overall roadmap completion
- 🔄 Rebuild plans dynamically as their profile changes
- 📈 Track long-term progress over months and years
